WHY PSYBER?

We’re not AAA.
That’s the point.

PsyberGamesStudio was founded solo with one rule: make the game we’re dying to play, even if no one asked for it. One founder. One vision. No middlemen.

No publishers. No crunch. No loot boxes. We build slow, atmospheric games that respect your time and your intelligence — inspired by Anatomy, Signalis, Outer Wilds and late-night radio static.

  • ✓ Built primarily in Unity — solo-dev friendly
  • ✓ Original synth scores & lo-fi sound design
  • ✓ Lore drops via devlogs, not DLC roadmaps — community helps shape the games
Meet the team ↓

◈ Design Pillars

Atmosphere over action. Loneliness as a mechanic. Every environment tells a story.

Unity • Blender (3D) • Pyxel Edit & Clip Studio Paint (2D) • Hand-crafted shaders • Synth / lo-fi scores — production varies per project, rooted in late-80s synth & early-90s nostalgia

COMING SOON • 2026 • GOOGLE PLAY

Twitch Out

High-stakes simplicity. Tap the vanishing light before it disappears. With every successful tap the light vanishes faster — until you reach the Twitch Point, the boundary of human reaction speed.

Built around an immediate, accessible one-tap loop with competitive progression to drive replayability.

  • ⚡ Single & Double Light Modes — classic reflex training + dual-light challenge
  • 🎨 13 Unlockable Colours — customise your light with coins earned in-game or via optional in-app purchases
  • 🏆 Live Leaderboards — integrated rankings to challenge for top positions
  • 👑 Prestige Hierarchy — Twitch King & Twitch Demon skill titles with shield and challenge mechanics to defend or claim #1

“Coming Soon: Twitch Out from Psyber Games Studios” — featured on Scottish Games Network

Twitch Out is in final stages of production and will be available on Google Play Store in the coming weeks.

RiftRiders

Ride the Rift. Players ride as a Rift Rider through the rift portal, collecting power-ups and coins to expand the rift board inventories and unlock new playable content.

Originally released on Google Play Store in 2021, RiftRiders has since been discontinued — a legacy title that laid the foundation for Psyber Games Studio.

  • 🌀 Endless rift-running through the portal
  • 🔋 Collect power-ups & coins mid-ride
  • 🧩 Expand rift board inventories
  • 🔓 Unlock new playable content

Future Plans: Perhaps one day RiftRiders will be redeveloped and relaunched with a whole bunch of newly added functionality.
